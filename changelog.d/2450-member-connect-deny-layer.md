### Fixed
- Ordinary members can now connect their own brokerage: the member role gains `integration:connect` (blanket grant, #2450), with a new per-user denial layer letting a tenant-admin revoke `integration:connect` for one member in their tenant — deny-beats-grant, checked before role grants — while every other member keeps it, and a clear route restores it. Deny/clear routes (`POST /api/iaaa/permission/deny`, `POST /api/iaaa/permission/clear`) follow the grant/revoke patterns (tenant-admin + role:assign authority, step-up + audit-before-allow, self-deny/self-clear forbidden, granter attribution from the session). `integration:use`/`manage` stay admin-held; platform-admin gains no tenant permission. The revoke control's UI belongs to the user management module (#2462).
