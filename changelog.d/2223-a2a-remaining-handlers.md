### Added

- Remaining A2A handlers (#2223 final slice): `POST /a2a/screener`, `/a2a/scheduled`, `/a2a/research`, `/a2a/report-asset`, `/a2a/browser`, and `/a2a/slack` now serve their agents' real operations through per-agent dispatch modules in the TradingAgent/slice-1 shape, completing the #2223 programme — every Think agent is reachable over A2A, and every card advertises exactly the operations its dispatch serves (anti-drift pinned). Instance discipline follows the survey: scheduled was already resolved to the live "default" instance; screener lands on the service instance "default" and research on `research:<service-login>`, both failing closed when no service identity is configured (runs are attributed to the service identity explicitly — the research methods' legacy "default" login fallback is never inherited); report-asset lands on "default" with identity carried in the request. Browser and slack delivery targets are never taken from A2A arguments — a caller-supplied `webhookUrl` is stripped, so snaps and approval tickets go only to env-configured destinations. Slack's HTTP ingress is not exposed over A2A (it keeps its signature gate behind the worker routes); this slice is also SlackAgent's first production instantiation as a Durable Object. Report-asset fetch returns metadata plus base64 bytes, and a generation failure (the method's null signal) surfaces as an honest failure, never a success.
