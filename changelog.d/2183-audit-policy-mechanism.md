### 2183 — Q7 audit-policy mechanism, proposal slice (#2183)

- **Q7 audit-policy mechanism, proposal slice (#2183, sub-issue of #2012, for CISO+CDO review under #945)**: port-first `AuditPolicyPort` (Drizzle D1 + in-memory adapters, migration 0010) with the blocking set, enforcement mode, and retention period as externalized operator data — seeded PROPOSED (money actions `payment.charge` / `payment.refund` / `subscription.change` / `credit.issue` block, reads never block, retention 365 days) and pending CISO+CDO sign-off. Enforcement defaults to audit-only/log mode and NEVER blocks until sign-off plus an explicit operator enablement; a SHA-256 hash chain (`prev_hash` + `payload_hash` + action + timestamp) gives tamper evidence over D1 chain rows, with verification detecting modified, deleted-middle, and reordered rows, and operator-invoked retention prune that prunes nothing when the period is unset or invalid. No money-path rewiring, no `mas_events` change, no enforcement activation, draft only.
