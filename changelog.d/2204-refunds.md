### Added
- Refund request + operator approval + gateway execution (#2204, implementation of #2147, main-based rebuild superseding #2298): a refund is requested (recorded with reason, amount, and original payment reference), approved or denied by a store operator (operator HITL only — no automatic refunds), and executed via PaymentGatewayService.executeRefund with the stored Stripe payment-intent id. Subscription payment-intent ids resolve from the subscription row (#2200); a legacy row with no stored payment-intent id, and (until the native ledger slice #2194 wires its run→PI lookup) a pay-per-run charge with no resolvable id, fail closed with a clear error — never a guessed id. Refund policy is externalized operator data (migration 0018, seeded with proposed defaults: 30-day window, operator-HITL approver, full-only refunds, full subscription refund revokes the period immediately and removes the plan's included runs, partial subscription refund keeps access, pay-per-run and credit effects none) and changeable without a code deploy. Crypto (x402) payments are non-refundable by default; a compensating operator payout is a separate owner decision and is not built here. Executed refunds post the balanced journal effect (Dr 5000 / Cr 1000) best-effort and are audited. No live refund path activates: execution sits behind the monetization flag and the approver gate, and the flag stays OFF.
