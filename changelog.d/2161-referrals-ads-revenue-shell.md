Share-button skins stay as-is (third-party network colors, already token-valued). Per-ad accent colors remain record data flowing through Card/Badge style props.
- Card and Badge gain a generic `style` prop for data-driven styling (per-ad accents); props stay in Chat since categories, external ads, tables, and ads are shared across these pages and with nlq — this slice moves rendering, not state.
