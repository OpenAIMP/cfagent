### Added
- Journal subscription / deferred-revenue mapping (#2155 follow-up, stacked rebuild on #2236 superseding #2205): new liability account 2100 Deferred revenue (seeded as data by ensureJournalSchema, no migration — the chart seed is data, not schema), and a subscription purchase mapping of Dr 1000 Funds received / Cr 2100 Deferred revenue. A purchase is NOT immediate revenue (no 4000 credit). The purchase route posts best-effort after activation succeeds (same postJournalBestEffort pattern as charge/credit/redemption: a journal failure is loudly logged and never fails the purchase), and posts nothing on any refusal path. The RECOGNITION schedule (deferred → earned over the subscription period) is a follow-up slice and is deliberately not built here: no recognition sweeper, no cron, no amortization logic. The JournalPort shape is unchanged, so a future real-GL adapter still swaps in behind the same port.
