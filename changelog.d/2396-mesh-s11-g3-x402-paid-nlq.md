### Changed
- Agent Mesh S11 (#2396, programme #2225 G3): the NLQ paid leg no longer records simulated receipts. `paid_scan` / `paid_research` in the NLQ executor previously ran the premium service for free and booked a self-minted receipt (status "simulated") into the payments ledger; the executor has no payment-proof channel (the NLQ plan schema carries no proof field), so both executors now refuse paid actions honestly — nothing executed, nothing recorded — naming the premium endpoint (`/api/premium/options-scan`, `/api/premium/market-research`) where a real x402 payment is collected, verified on-chain, and recorded from the settlement. The synchronous executor previously had no paid-action branch and answered paid plans with conversation history; it now returns the same explicit refusal. Already-recorded simulated rows are not deleted or rewritten — they stay in the ledger labelled `simulated` — but wallet status aggregation no longer counts them toward earned/spent totals, transaction count, or the derived balance. Verified paths (premium routes, PaymentsAgent) are unchanged; no price, flag, or network behavior changed.
