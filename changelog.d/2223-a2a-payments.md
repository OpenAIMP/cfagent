### Added

- PaymentsAgent A2A door (#2223 payments slice): `POST /a2a/payments` now serves the agent's real operations — `payments.budget_check`, `payments.today_spent`, `payments.list`, and `payments.process` — through a dispatch module in the TradingAgent/slice-1 shape, giving `processPaidRequest` its first production caller (user decision on #2223): arguments pass through faithfully, and the method's own facilitator verification and ledger recording remain the only money path (a verification failure or budget denial is a classified failure result and records nothing; a budget-check denial is a successful check result, matching the method's fail-closed design). The payments card is re-scoped to exactly these four operations — `payments.draft` is removed from it, as drafts are a MAS tool served by the orchestrator card.
