Successful-path behaviour, statuses, approval rules, and weight math are unchanged (characterization-pinned); the routes pass the DO storage (`ctx.getStorage()`) to turn the wrap on, and unregistered/absent storage keeps the sequential legacy execution. Sabotage pins prove a throw at any leg (apply, record, status) leaves the pre-state exactly intact.
