### Added

- **Workflow Runs tab** (`?tab=workflow-runs`, #2095): a single ledger of every workflow execution the job engine recorded — one row per job chain or standalone outcome, with the run's trigger and its evidence, status, duration, stage progress, and a result summary. Run detail shows every stage in order (`feed → gather → generate → store → serve → deliver` or the capability itself), its status/timing, the recorded reason a skipped/held/failed stage ended that way, whether the stage came from a job row or a stage transition, normalized inputs/outputs, artifact links, and per-channel delivery states. Reads `GET /api/workflow-runs` (`src/services/workflowRuns.ts`, `src/agents/routes/workflowRuns.ts`); built only from `ui/` primitives with token-only styling. Honest by construction: unknown triggers render "Not recorded", runs without stage records say so, and raw payloads/secrets never leave the Durable Object.
