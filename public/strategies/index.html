Option strategies

  • Long Call — Buy one call; defined risk, unlimited upside.
  • Long Put — Buy one put; defined risk, large downside payoff.
  • Short Call — Sell one naked call; unlimited upside risk.
  • Short Put — Sell one naked put; downside risk is strike minus premium.
  • Cash-Secured Put — Sell one put backed by cash for assignment; risk is strike minus premium.
  • LEAPS Call — Long call with at least 365 DTE (stock replacement).
  • LEAPS Put — Long put with at least 365 DTE.
  • Covered Call — Long 100 shares plus one short call.
  • Covered Put — Short 100 shares plus one short put; unlimited upside risk.
  • Protective Put — Long 100 shares plus one long put.
  • Protective Call — Short 100 shares plus one long call.
  • Protective Collar — Long 100 shares, long put below, short call above.
  • Covered Strangle — Long 100 shares, short put below, short call above.
  • Covered Short Straddle — Long 100 shares plus short straddle (short call + short put at same strike).
  • Call Ratio Write — Long 100 shares plus two short calls; one call is uncovered.
  • Put Ratio Write — Short 100 shares plus two short puts.
  • Call Debit Spread — Buy lower call, sell higher call.
  • Put Debit Spread — Buy higher put, sell lower put.
  • Call Credit Spread — Sell lower call, buy higher call.
  • Put Credit Spread — Sell higher put, buy lower put.
  • Long Straddle — Buy call and put at the same strike.
  • Short Straddle — Sell call and put at the same strike; unlimited risk.
  • Long Strangle — Buy lower put and higher call.
  • Short Strangle — Sell lower put and higher call; unlimited risk.
  • Long Guts — Buy in-the-money call and put (call strike below put strike).
  • Short Guts — Sell in-the-money call and put; unlimited risk.
  • Strip — Buy 1 ATM call and 2 ATM puts; volatility play with bearish bias.
  • Strap — Buy 2 ATM calls and 1 ATM put; volatility play with bullish bias.
  • Long Call Butterfly — Buy 1 / sell 2 / buy 1 calls with equal wings.
  • Short Call Butterfly — Sell 1 / buy 2 / sell 1 calls with equal wings.
  • Long Put Butterfly — Buy 1 / sell 2 / buy 1 puts with equal wings.
  • Short Put Butterfly — Sell 1 / buy 2 / sell 1 puts with equal wings.
  • Iron Butterfly — Short straddle with long wings (equal widths).
  • Reverse Iron Butterfly — Long straddle with short wings (equal widths).
  • Broken-Wing Butterfly — 1 / -2 / 1 butterfly with unequal wings (call and put forms).
  • Inverse Call Broken Wing — Sell 1 lower call, buy 2 middle calls, sell 1 higher call with unequal wings.
  • Inverse Put Broken Wing — Sell 1 lower put, buy 2 middle puts, sell 1 higher put with unequal wings.
  • Skip-Strike Butterfly — Butterfly where one wing skips a strike (one wing is exactly twice the other).
  • Unbalanced Butterfly — Ratio butterfly with uneven quantities: 1 / -3 / 2 (call and put forms).
  • Long Call Condor — Buy outer calls, sell inner calls, equal wings.
  • Short Call Condor — Sell outer calls, buy inner calls, equal wings.
  • Long Put Condor — Buy outer puts, sell inner puts, equal wings.
  • Short Put Condor — Sell outer puts, buy inner puts, equal wings.
  • Iron Condor — Put credit spread plus call credit spread.
  • Reverse Iron Condor — Put debit spread plus call debit spread.
  • Unbalanced Condor — Condor with unequal wing widths (call and put forms).
  • Call Calendar Spread — Sell near call, buy far call at the same strike.
  • Put Calendar Spread — Sell near put, buy far put at the same strike.
  • Double Calendar Spread — Put calendar below plus call calendar above.
  • Call Diagonal Spread — Buy far call at lower strike, sell near call at higher strike.
  • Put Diagonal Spread — Buy far put at higher strike, sell near put at lower strike.
  • Double Diagonal Spread — Put diagonal below plus call diagonal above.
  • Poor Man's Covered Call — Deep ITM far-dated call (180+ DTE) financed by a near-dated OTM short call.
  • Poor Man's Covered Put — Deep ITM far-dated put (180+ DTE) financed by a near-dated OTM short put.
  • Ratio Call Spread — Buy 1 lower call, sell 2 higher calls; unlimited upside risk.
  • Ratio Put Spread — Buy 1 higher put, sell 2 lower puts.
  • Call Backspread — Sell 1 lower call, buy 2 higher calls.
  • Put Backspread — Sell 1 higher put, buy 2 lower puts.
  • Call Ladder — Buy lower call, sell two higher calls at different strikes; unlimited upside risk.
  • Bear Call Ladder — Sell 1 lower call, buy 1 middle call, buy 1 higher call; upside breakout play.
  • Put Ladder — Buy higher put, sell two lower puts at different strikes.
  • Bull Put Ladder — Sell 1 higher put, buy 1 middle put, buy 1 lower put; downside breakout play.
  • Jade Lizard — Short put plus short call spread above.
  • Reverse Jade Lizard — Short OTM call plus bull put spread below; eliminates downside risk when net credit exceeds put spread width.
  • Big Lizard — Short straddle plus long OTM call above.
  • Seagull — Bullish seagull: long call spread financed by a short put below.
  • Synthetic Long Stock — Long call plus short put at the same strike.
  • Synthetic Short Stock — Short call plus long put at the same strike; unlimited upside risk.
  • Risk Reversal — Bullish form: short OTM put financing a long OTM call.
  • Box Spread — Bull call spread plus bear put spread on the same strikes (long box).
  • Short Box Spread — Reverse of the long box (synthetic borrowing).
  • Conversion — Long 100 shares, long put, short call at the same strike.
  • Reverse Conversion — Short 100 shares, short put, long call at the same strike.
